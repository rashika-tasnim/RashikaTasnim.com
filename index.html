About Me

I am a developer, data analyst, and computer science researcher with experience building accessible web applications, interactive prototypes, and digital training tools. My work spans web, mobile, VR, and AR development, as well as the analysis and visualisation of behavioural and operational data. I apply principles of universal design, accessibility, usability, and inclusive technology throughout my development and evaluation work.


My experience includes:


  • Building responsive web applications using React, Next.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and Supabase.
  • Developing accessible and multimodal interfaces, interactive prototypes, and mobile, VR, and AR applications.
  • Evaluating functionality, accessibility, usability, and regression through manual and automated testing.
  • Cleaning, analysing, and visualising behavioural and operational data using Python, SQL, databases, and data-visualisation tools.
